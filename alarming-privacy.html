Alarming – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026 · Last updated: 14 October 2026

Alarming is an Android app with alarms, reminders, a timer, a stopwatch and a focus mode (the “app”). It is made by an independent developer (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the app does with information. The short version: the app does not collect your personal data, and nothing you enter leaves your phone.

In short

  • No account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • The app does not send any data to us or to anyone else. It makes no network requests.
  • Everything you create (alarms, reminders, settings, sounds) is stored only on your phone.
  • The app does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or phone number.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes everything it stored.

1. Information we collect

We collect none. We have no servers for the app, so there is nowhere for your data to be sent, and we do not receive, see or have access to anything you do in the app.

2. Information stored on your device

To work, the app keeps the following in its private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read it, and it is not uploaded anywhere by the app:

  • Your alarms, reminders, timers, clocks and focus blocks, including the names, descriptions, times, dates and repeat days you type.
  • Your settings: language, time format, first day of the week, theme, style, and whether the welcome screens were shown.
  • Custom ringtones you create. If you pick an audio file from your phone, the app trims it, saves the trimmed copy and keeps a copy of the original file inside the app so you can edit it later. The app reads only the files you choose.

Your phone’s own backup feature (for example Android’s backup to your Google account) may include some app data, depending on your device settings. That is done by Android and Google under your own account settings, not by us.

3. Permissions the app asks for, and why

Each permission is used only for the purpose below, on your device. None of them is used to collect or send data.

PermissionWhy the app needs it
NotificationsTo show alarm, reminder, timer and focus notifications.
Exact alarmsTo ring and notify at the exact time you set.
Full-screen notificationsTo open the alarm screen over the lock screen when an alarm rings.
Display over other appsTo show the alarm screen on top of whatever app is open.
Wake lockTo wake the phone and keep it awake while an alarm rings.
Foreground service (media playback)To keep the alarm sound playing reliably until you dismiss or snooze it.
VibrationTo vibrate when an alarm rings.
Run at startupTo set your alarms again after the phone restarts or the time or time zone changes.
Do Not Disturb access (optional)So alarms can ring even when Do Not Disturb or silent mode is on. You choose whether to allow it in system settings.
Battery optimization (optional)The app opens the system screen where you can let it run without battery restrictions, so alarms are not delayed. The app only opens that screen; you decide.

You can turn any of these off in your phone’s settings at any time. Alarms may then not ring reliably.

4. Sharing of information

We do not share, sell, rent or transfer any information to third parties, because we do not collect any. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking libraries. It is built with open-source frameworks (such as React Native and Expo) that run entirely on your device.

5. Google Play

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect its own information about installs, updates and, if you have opted in on your device, crash and performance data. That is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy and is not collected by the app itself.

6. Children

The app is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

7. Security and retention

Your data stays in the app’s private storage on your phone, protected by Android’s app sandbox. It is kept until you delete an item in the app, clear the app’s data, or uninstall the app, at which point it is removed. We hold no copy and so cannot recover it.

8. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on request. You are in full control of the data in the app: you can edit or delete it inside the app at any time.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example by adding a feature that uses the internet), we will update this page and the date above before the change reaches you, and the app’s Play Store listing will be updated to match.
